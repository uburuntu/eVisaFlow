## Store identity

Name: eVisaFlow

Subtitle: Encrypted offline eVisa organiser

Opening description:

> eVisaFlow is an independent, unofficial app that helps you securely organise
> previously generated eVisa proofs and share codes for offline access, printing, and
> sharing. Guided automation may be available during an authorised private beta.
>
> eVisaFlow is not operated by, affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by the UK
> Government, Home Office, UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI), or GOV.UK. The official
> GOV.UK eVisa and share-code service is free.

Official source:

`https://www.gov.uk/evisa/view-evisa-get-share-code-prove-immigration-status`

> have authority to act for this person.

## Saved-copy and travel disclosure

> A saved copy is a convenience record. It does not replace your passport or travel
> document, the official UKVI record, or live verification by a carrier, employer,
> landlord, or border official.

When offline:

> This saved copy has not been checked against the live UKVI record since [date].

Use separate labels:

- Saved on this phone: [date]
- Last checked online: [date]
- Share code valid until: [date]
- Available offline

Never describe the saved copy as current, official, guaranteed, always valid, or
accepted at the border.

## Proposed paywall

Heading: Keep every proof ready, even offline

Proposed benefits, subject to final product approval:

- securely organise up to six people;
- refresh share codes in a guided sequence;
- receive local expiry and travel reminders;
- keep encrypted offline copies on this phone;
- create a printable emergency summary;
- share and print saved proofs quickly.

Required footer:

> Pro pays for eVisaFlow convenience features, including automation, encrypted
> organisation, offline storage, reminders, sharing, and printing. It does not pay for
> an eVisa, immigration status, a government share code, or access to the official
> GOV.UK service. The official service is free.

Commands:

- Start Pro
- Restore purchases
- Continue with free features
- Open the free official GOV.UK service

Do not use per-result purchase language, "premium eVisa", "official proof",
"unlimited government checks", or a promise of guaranteed access or current status.
